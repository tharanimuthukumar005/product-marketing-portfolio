# WhatsApp-First Conversion Strategy

## Problem
Users add products to cart but do not complete the purchase.
Email reminders have low open rates.

## Solution
Use WhatsApp for faster and direct communication.

## Execution
- Send WhatsApp message after cart abandonment
- Example:
  "Hey! You left something in your cart 😊 Complete now & get 10% off!"
- Add urgency: "Offer valid for 2 hours"
- Send follow-up message if no response

## Metrics
- Open rate
- Conversion rate
- Cart recovery rate

## Outcome
Higher engagement and improved conversions compared to email.
