# Referral Based Growth Strategy

## Problem
Customer acquisition through ads is expensive.

## Solution
Encourage users to refer others with incentives.

## Execution
- After purchase:
  "Invite your friend & both get ₹100 OFF"
- Provide referral link
- Enable sharing via WhatsApp/social media

## Metrics
- Referral rate
- New users acquired
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

## Outcome
Organic growth and reduced marketing cost.
