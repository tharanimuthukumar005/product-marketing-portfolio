# How to Withdraw Money from an ATM

## Overview
An Automated Teller Machine (ATM) allows bank customers to perform basic banking transactions without visiting a bank branch. Using an ATM, customers can withdraw cash, check their account balance, and deposit money. 

## Prerequisites
Before using an ATM to withdraw money, ensure the following:

1. You have an active bank account.
2. You have a valid ATM or debit card issued by your bank.
3. You remember your ATM Personal Identification Number (PIN).

## Steps to Withdraw Money

1. Insert your ATM card into the ATM machine.
2. Select the **"Withdraw Cash"** option from the menu.
3. Choose your account type (Savings or Current).
4. Enter your ATM PIN for authentication.
5. Enter the amount you want to withdraw.
6. Confirm the transaction by selecting **"OK"**.
7. Collect the dispensed cash.
8. Take the receipt (if printed) and remove your ATM card.

## Example
For example, a customer can insert their ATM card and enter their PIN to withdraw ₹2000 instantly instead of waiting in a queue at the bank.

## Conclusion
ATM machines provide a convenient and efficient way for bank customers to withdraw cash, check their account balance, and perform basic banking transactions without visiting a bank branch.
